# InsulaCRM - Functionality Overview

> **For Real Estate Wholesalers, Investors & Agents**  
> A complete, self-hosted CRM built to solve the exact problems real estate firms face every day.

**Date:** 2026-10-30  
**Version:** Based on codebase analysis  
**Type:** Multi-tenant Laravel 12 CRM

---

## The Problem This Solves

Running a real estate wholesaling or brokerage business means juggling motivated seller leads, property analysis, deal pipelines, buyers, follow-ups, and team coordination - all while trying to close deals profitably. Most CRMs are either too generic (Salesforce, HubSpot) or too expensive and bloated for real estate operations.

**InsulaCRM solves this by giving you a purpose-built system that actually understands how real estate deals work.**

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Lead Management - Never Lose a Motivated Seller

| Feature | What It Does | Business Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Centralized Lead Inbox** | Capture leads from cold calls, direct mail, driving for dollars, PPC, SEO, webforms, referrals, and more | One source of truth for every seller lead |
| **Smart Lead Scoring** | Dual scoring system - automated system score + AI-powered motivation score shown side-by-side | Instantly identify the hottest, most motivated sellers worth pursuing first |
| **Lead Pipeline Tracking** | 14 customizable statuses from "New" to "Closed Won/Lost" | Know exactly where every lead stands in your acquisition process |
| **DNC Protection** | Do Not Contact flag with system-wide enforcement | Stay compliant and avoid costly mistakes |
| **Lead Photos & Notes** | Attach photos, detailed notes, and track all interactions in one place | Full context on every property before making offers |
| **Contact Normalization** | Auto-formats phone numbers for WhatsApp with country code detection | One-click communication from anywhere |
| **Custom Lead Sources** | Add your own sources to track ROI per marketing channel | Know which marketing actually brings deals |

### 2. Property & Deal Analysis - Know Your Numbers Instantly

| Feature | What It Does | Business Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Property Tracking** | Link properties to leads with address, beds/baths/sqft, year built, lot size, property type | Complete property intel at your fingertips |
| **Distress Indicators** | Track tax delinquent, pre-foreclosure, probate, code violations, vacant, etc. | Spot the most motivated and desperate sellers faster |
| **Deal Calculator** | Built-in ARV, Repair Costs, MAO (Max Allowable Offer) calculations | Calculate offers in seconds with confidence, protect your margins |
| **Financial Analysis** | Track contract price, assignment fee, earnest money, closing costs | Never guess on profit - see numbers clearly |
| **Address Normalization** | Deduplicates properties to prevent chasing the same seller twice | Save time and avoid embarrassing double-contact |
| **Document Storage** | Upload contracts, photos, inspection reports, title docs per deal | Keep all deal paperwork organized and accessible |

### 3. Deal Pipeline & Kanban - Close More Deals

| Feature | What It Does | Business Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Visual Kanban Board** | Accordion-style pipeline with drag-and-drop between stages | See your entire pipeline at a glance, move deals with one drag |
| **Due Diligence Tracking** | Countdown timer with 48-hour urgency alerts | Never miss critical deadlines that kill deals |
| **Deal Activity Feed** | Complete timeline of every call, text, email, note, and action | Full deal history for smooth handoffs between team members |
| **Inline Deal Editing** | Quick-edit contract price, EM, inspection period, closing date without leaving board | Update deals on the fly without breaking workflow |
| **Pipeline Analytics** | Track deal count, total value, fees per stage | Spot bottlenecks and forecast closings accurately |
| **Role-Based Views** | Acquisition vs Disposition agents see what's relevant to them | Focus on their job, eliminate clutter |

### 4. Disposition - Find Buyers & Get Deals Assigned

| Feature | What It Does | Business Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Disposition Room** | Dedicated workspace to match deals with your cash buyer list | Close deals faster by instantly connecting to buyers |
| **Buyer Management** | Store buyer info, buying criteria (price range, locations, property types), cash status, verification | Build a qualified buyers list you can actually close with |
| **Buyer Matching** | System helps match deals to buyers based on their criteria | Send the right deals to the right buyers, every time |
| **Buyer Portal** | Public portal where buyers can view available deals | Let buyers browse your inventory 24/7 without constant texts/emails |
| **Assignment Fee Tracking** | Track assignment fees per deal with full visibility | Maximize profits and never lose track of money owed |
| **Buyer Notifications** | Notify buyers of new matching deals automatically | Keep buyers engaged and get faster responses |
